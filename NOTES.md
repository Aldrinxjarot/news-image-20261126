# Story list — Thursday, 26 November 2026

Every story was checked against wire-style coverage dated Thursday
26 November 2026 before it was drawn. Where an event itself belongs
to an earlier day, the page says that day in words or the item was
dropped. The page was drawn on Thursday (WIB) from the day's
reporting so far.

## Used on the page

1. **HERO — US unveils tariffs on 14 partners; Asia's markets
   bounce back (Reuters, Thursday).** Reuters' global-markets wrap
   dated 26 Nov 2026: White House proposals for new tariffs on 14
   major trading partners including China, Japan and the European
   Union, under a probe into unfair trade practices, with the new
   duties not stacking on July's. The USTR proposed a higher rate
   of 25% for South Korea, and Seoul said on Thursday it would hold
   further talks to safeguard its national interests (Reuters,
   Thu 26 Nov 03:01 UTC). After Wall Street recouped most of its
   losses, Asian shares rallied on Thursday — the chart's figures
   are the wrap's: Nikkei +1.6%, South Korea's Kospi +1.9%,
   Asia-Pacific shares outside Japan +0.6%, S&P 500 futures +0.5%.
2. **Indonesia — Pongkor mine search resumes (Reuters,
   Thursday).** Reuters, 26 Nov: rescuers resume the search on
   Thursday for 13 workers trapped underground in an illegal gold
   mine in Bogor, West Java, with 13 already confirmed dead. The
   26 workers entered the mine on Monday, when heavy rains
   inundated the shaft and left them trapped 100 m underground;
   six of the dead have been identified. This is Wednesday's panel
   story with Thursday's development — the confirmed toll and the
   resumed search — and the page dates the flooding to Monday.
3. **Europe — EU leaders clash over the budget (Reuters,
   Thursday).** Reuters, 26 Nov: EU leaders meet on Thursday over
   the bloc's nearly €1.6 trillion budget proposal for 2028–34
   (€1.582 trillion in Council President Costa's latest text),
   hoping to narrow differences toward a deal at the 17–18
   December summit. The budget includes up to €100 billion for
   Ukraine; a fight on Thursday is over whether all member
   countries should contribute to a proposed €45 billion loan to
   Ukraine, which Slovakia, the Czech Republic, Hungary and
   Bulgaria object to.
4. **Asia — ASEAN+3 digital payments (Reuters, Thursday).**
   Reuters, 26 Nov: China, Japan and South Korea agreed with
   Southeast Asian nations on Thursday to find ways to better
   connect payment systems and mesh regulatory measures for
   digital assets across their borders. Deputies to finance
   ministers and central bank governors of ASEAN+3 struck the
   deal for a dedicated working group at a meeting in Kyoto, and
   flagged a new emergency lending mechanism under the region's
   $240 billion Chiang Mai Initiative safety net.
5. **Technology — Nvidia halts shipments to Southeast Asia (FT,
   reported by Reuters, Thursday).** Reuters, 26 Nov: Nvidia has
   told its manufacturing partners to stop shipping servers to
   four Southeast Asian countries, the Financial Times reported
   on Thursday; partners including Dell and Supermicro told
   buyers in Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Indonesia that
   orders will not be shipped. The page carries the honest label:
   Reuters could not immediately verify the report.
